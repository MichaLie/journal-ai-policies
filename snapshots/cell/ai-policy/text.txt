Authors preparing a manuscript for submission can use generative AI tools, including agents and deep research tools, to synthesize complex literature, provide an overview of a field or research question, identify research gaps, generate ideas, and provide tailored support for tasks such as content organization and improving language and readability, within the limitations described on the Elsevier policy page. The declaration does not apply to the use of basic tools, such as tools used to check grammar, spelling, and references. If you have nothing to disclose, you do not need to add a statement. Allows use of generative AI tools in the creation of explanatory images, in line with Elsevier policy. Usage must be declared in the relevant figure legend and general AI disclosure statement, with details of the specific tool, version, and usage. Does not permit use of generative AI or AI-assisted tools in the production of cover art. An editorial exception may be granted only when the use of AI or AI-assisted tools are integral to the research described in the manuscript. General-purpose generative AI image tools may not be used in creation of graphical abstracts.
