# November pre-release review (20–21 November 2026)

Prepared against 9fcffe3. This review checked every record before the repository was shared more widely.

## What was checked

- All HTTP sources were re-fetched on 20 November 2026. Seven pages changed; five of the changes were navigation only. ISC's consultation moved on (round 2 closed, round 3 planned for January 2027). Elsevier's editors section had changed after its last sign-off.
- All browser and archive sources were read on the live page on 20 November 2026, and every stored quote was found there. Nine browser extracts were re-captured as full AI sections, each checked against the live page by SHA-256 before saving.
- Each record was audited against its stored sources, corrected, then re-checked by an independent reviewer. Claimed errors went to three reviewers who tried to refute them, and a record was corrected only when a majority upheld the error. This loop ran up to three rounds per record.
- Coding rules are now written down in [schema/coding-conventions.md](../schema/coding-conventions.md).

## What changed

- About 50 coding errors were corrected. Examples: ACS reviewer use had been coded as a ban; Science image use had been coded as allowed; several conditions stated facts no stored source contains.
- Coverage was added where sources clearly address an activity but the record had omitted it.
- Eleven organisations were added: NIH, UKRI, NHMRC, Horizon Europe, bioRxiv/medRxiv (openRxiv), Royal Society Publishing, Oxford University Press, NEJM, The Lancet, Wolters Kluwer Health and ASM Journals.
- One contradiction was recorded (ICMJE: the reviewer page lets journals authorise uploading a manuscript to AI, the editor page says only authors can). One new dating oddity was recorded (UKRI).
- "Licensing published papers for AI training" now covers only rules on using published content to train AI. Advice to authors about their own material in AI tools (Elsevier, The Lancet, Wiley, MDPI, ISTIC) moved to those organisations' notes.
- Monitoring changes: refetched extracts are committed only when the quote gate passes; an extract that shrinks sharply or loses a cited quote is held for a person; the monthly issue is reused instead of duplicated. The site now shows absolute check dates.
- A usage limit cut short one verification round. The four records it left unchecked (Elsevier, Springer Nature, ISTIC, Wiley) were re-checked on 21 November; no coding errors were found, and omitted rules and exemptions were added.

## Still open

- Most sources have no maintainer sign-off (`adjudicated_sha256`). Five sources changed since sign-off: three Elsevier pages, the Springer Nature peer-review page (fuller capture), and Wiley's guidelines (navigation only).
- BMJ and Sage block scripted fetches; their stored text dates from 11 November and was confirmed on the live page in a browser on 20 November.
- Not yet covered: journal-level rules under most delegating publishers, NWO, SNSF, Canada's Tri-agency, Karger, Thieme, the Company of Biologists, Rockefeller University Press and others. The Czech funders checked (GA ČR, TA ČR, AZV ČR 2027 documents) contain no AI provision; they are not yet recorded.
