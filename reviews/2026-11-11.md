## Deliberate model boundary

This PR uses task-specific conditions and source/audience records rather than introducing a new multi-dimensional schema in the same change. Recipient/timing, imagery subtype, tool authorization and research-versus-writing differences are described in the conditions. A future schema migration should preserve these distinctions, version the exported data contract and test the grid before replacing existing fields. The monitor’s raw hashes and manual-review obligations are unchanged; policy-body diffing and linked-page discovery are follow-up engineering work, not claimed as completed here.
